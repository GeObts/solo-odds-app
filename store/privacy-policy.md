# Privacy Policy — Solo Mining Odds

_Last updated: May 29, 2026_

Solo Mining Odds ("the app") is a Bitcoin solo-mining odds calculator published
by BasedMining. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what the app does
and does not do with data.

## We do not collect personal data
The app does not require an account, login, or wallet. We do not collect, store,
or transmit any personally identifiable information about you. We have no servers
that receive your data, and we do not use analytics or advertising SDKs.

## Information stored on your device
The app saves a single preference locally on your device: your chosen theme
(dark or light). This never leaves your device and is removed if you uninstall
the app.

## Network requests
To show live figures, the app fetches **public, non-personal** data from:

- **api.basedmining.xyz** — public Bitcoin network and BasedMining pool statistics
- **mempool.space** — the current Bitcoin (BTC) price

These requests retrieve public information only. The app does not send any
personal information in these requests. Any hashrate or address you type stays on
your device and is used only for the on-screen calculation; it is not transmitted
to us.

## Data sharing
We do not sell, rent, or share any user data with third parties, because we do
not collect any.

## Children
The app is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children.

## Changes
We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected
by the "Last updated" date above.
