Solo Mining Odds is a fast, clean calculator that shows your real chances of
finding a Bitcoin block on your own — using live network difficulty, network
hashrate, and BasedMining pool data.

Enter your hashrate (or drag the slider from MH/s up to PH/s) and instantly see:

• Your solo odds per block — the headline "1 in N" number
• Your odds per day and an estimated time-to-block
• A Jackpot Meter showing how close you are to an expected win
• A 30-day Win Simulator that role-plays a month of mining — blocks found,
  total earnings in BTC and USD, and your best/worst luck streaks
• Live network stats: hashrate, difficulty, block height, block reward, BTC price
• Live BasedMining pool stats: pool hashrate, active miners, and pool luck

Everything updates from live data, so the numbers reflect the network right now.

Pick the look you like — the app opens in a sleek dark theme and you can switch
to light mode with one tap. Share your odds with friends straight from the app.
